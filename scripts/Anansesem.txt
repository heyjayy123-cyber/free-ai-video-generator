Long ago, in a town called Nyankosem. Nana Nyankopon loved his people, but they were disobedient. He sent messengers, but they all failed.

---
IMAGE: Ananse the man with 4 spider arms wearing colorful kente fugu kneeling before king
Then Ananse came. He said, My Lord! Let me go! I, Ananse, can bring your people! Nyankopon said, if you succeed, I will grant you anything you wish!

---
IMAGE: Long dusty African road with baobab trees at sunset, Ananse walking alone with small gourd
Ananse set off. Three days journey. Very long. Night came. He begged for a place to sleep.

---
IMAGE: Inside African mud hut at night, broken gourd on floor, Ananse wailing loudly, scared family
At midnight, the gourd broke! Ananse wailed. This gourd was given to me by Nyankopon! Whoever breaks it, he will kill their household! The family said, Take our fattest chicken!

---
IMAGE: Cow pen with cows at night, chicken gone, Ananse arguing with farmer family in morning
Ananse took the fat chicken. That night the chicken disappeared with the cows. Ananse said, You have killed Nyankopon's chicken! They gave him their best cow.

---
IMAGE: Epic procession, Ananse carrying beautiful Ghanaian girl on back leading large crowd toward royal palace at sunset, celebration confetti
In the next town, Ananse tricked them and got the beautiful girl and all the people. He led them back to Nyankopon. The town was renamed from Nyankosem to Anansesem! The end.
